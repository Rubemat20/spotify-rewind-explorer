How do I get my Spotify Extended Streaming History?
  1. Go to spotify.com/account/privacy while logged in.
  2. Under "Download your data", request Extended streaming history (not the basic account data export -- that one doesn't include play-by-play history).
  3. Spotify emails you a link within a few days. Download the .zip.
  4. Drop that .zip right here, unopened -- no need to extract it first.

Everything above happens locally: files are read in memory by your browser and thrown away on refresh. This page has no server, no database, and no upload endpoint -- there is nothing for your data to be sent to.

Privacy & terms

Privacy. This page has no backend. Your streaming history is read by your browser's File API (and, for a .zip, the JSZip library) and processed entirely in memory on your device. Nothing is uploaded, logged, stored, or sent to any server -- there is no database and no upload endpoint for it to go to. Refreshing or closing the tab discards it. The only network requests this page makes are to Google Fonts and two CDN-hosted scripts (JSZip, html2canvas) -- never your data. No analytics, no cookies, no tracking.

Accuracy. Every number here (play counts, minutes, "top track," etc.) is this tool's own best-effort analysis of your export -- including judgment calls like folding a single's plays into its parent album, or grouping "Album (Deluxe)" with "Album." It won't always match Spotify's own official Wrapped numbers, which use different, undocumented methodology.
