## Accuracy

The statistics this tool produces -- play counts, minutes streamed, "top
track," era groupings, rising/cooling trends, and so on -- are this
project's own best-effort analysis of the export file, including several
judgment calls documented in the [README](README.md#how-it-works) (for
example, folding a single's plays into its parent album, or treating
"Album (Deluxe)" and "Album" as the same release). These numbers are
derived independently and may differ from Spotify's own official reporting
(including Spotify Wrapped), which uses different, undocumented
methodology. Nothing here is an official statistic from Spotify.
